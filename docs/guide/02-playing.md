# Playing a turn

## The action bar

The action bar sits above Foundry's hotbar. It shows the creature whose Token you control, or else your assigned character.

- **Counters**: your Action, Bonus Action, Reaction and movement, then pools such as Heroic Inspiration and Hit Dice.
- **Conditions**: shown with their stacks. Hover one for its rules and the time it has left.
- **Groups**: Actions, Bonus Actions, Reactions, Common, Items, then your spells by level.

To use an Action, target the creatures you mean with Foundry's targeting, then click its button.

- **Choices.** An Action with choices asks them first. The dialog offers only what you can pay, and previews what it spends, the dice and the save. A spell also has small slot buttons that cast it at that level directly.
- **Areas.**
  - For a sphere or other centred area, click where it centres.
  - For a cone or a line, click a corner on your Token's edge, then the direction.
  - Before you click, the creatures it would reach are outlined: allies in amber, others in red.
  - Escape or right-click cancels, and nothing is spent.
- **Greyed out.** Hover a greyed-out button to see why: no slot left, an item not equipped or attuned, or it can't be used now. Spells you haven't prepared don't appear at all.

**Common actions** are in the Common group: Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Grapple, Shove (Prone or Push), Unarmed Strike, Search, Study, Influence, Escape and Utilize. To Hide, you make a Stealth check against DC 15; on a success you become Invisible.

## Reactions

Each reaction on the bar has a toggle that cycles through three policies:

- **Ask** (the default): you are asked when it could be used.
- **Always**: it is used for you, without asking.
- **Never**: it is never offered.

A reaction is offered only when you can pay for it. Its prompt lists your options, with **Decline** chosen by default. Prompts have no time limit. If you're away, the GM can answer for you, and you'll see "The GM answered this for you."

**Opportunity Attacks** come built in. Every creature makes one with its first melee attack (or an Unarmed Strike) when a hostile creature leaves its reach during combat. Disengage prevents it. While you drag your Token, the ruler warns you of each Opportunity Attack your path would provoke. Its policy is in the Common group.

## After an Action

Each Action posts one chat card: one row per target with its outcome (Hit, Critical hit, Miss, Saved, Failed save, Immune), and the rolls. Damage and misses also float over the Tokens. Reactions post cards of their own. If an Action can't be done, only you see why.

**Odds.** When targets are chosen, attack and save buttons can show the odds before you act, such as "56% to hit (+4 vs AC 15, disadvantage)". The GM always sees them. Players see them if the GM turns on **Players see the odds**, or against a creature an effect has revealed to them.

## Dice

The GM picks digital or physical dice. With physical dice, you roll real dice and type in what they show:

- for a d20 test, the natural d20;
- with Advantage or Disadvantage, the higher or lower of your two d20s;
- for damage, one total for each damage type.

A result the dice couldn't have given is asked for again.
