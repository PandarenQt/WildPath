# Conditions and rules

WildPath plays by the 2024 rules unless your GM sets **Rules** to 2014. Conditions come from Actions, effects and the Token HUD; their rules apply on their own.

## Beyond the 2024 conditions

- **Bleeding** and **Envenomed**: damage at the start of each of your turns. Envenomed stacks.
- **Burning**: 1d4 Fire damage at the start of each of your turns, until the fire is put out (your GM removes it).
- **Cursed**: a curse is on you; its effect or its text says what it does. Remove Curse ends it.
- **Ethereal**: you are on the Ethereal Plane. You and creatures that are not can't target each other.
- **Half, Three-Quarters and Total Cover**: cover you have wherever you stand (inside a creature that swallowed you, for instance). Walls between you and an attacker may give more. A creature with Total Cover can't be targeted directly.
- **Surprised**: your initiative roll has Disadvantage, and rolling it ends this. Under the 2014 rules you instead can't move or act on your first turn, nor take a Reaction until it ends.
- **Gaseous** (Gaseous Form, Wind Walk): you are a cloud of mist and can't attack or cast spells.
- **Suffocating**: you can't breathe. You hold your breath for 1 + your Constitution modifier minutes (at least 3 turns); after that you gain a level of Exhaustion at the end of each of your turns. When you can breathe again, those levels go.
- **Confused**: at the start of each of your turns, your behavior for that turn is rolled and posted to chat.
- **Resurrection Sickness**: after Raise Dead, −4 to your D20 Tests; each Long Rest lifts 1.
- **Enlarged or Reduced**: your Token grows or shrinks one size while it lasts, and goes back afterwards.

## Difficult Terrain

Moving into Difficult Terrain costs twice the distance: 10 feet for a 5-foot square. Web, Spike Growth, Grease and similar spells make it, and your GM can paint it on the map. Freedom of Movement and a Ring of Free Action let you ignore it; Boots of the Winterlands ignore only ice and snow.

## Contagions

A contagion you catch shows on your sheet. Its symptoms start days later, as the world's time passes: Exhaustion, or Blindness for Sight Rot.

- **Cackle Fever**: after each Long Rest you save against it; three successes end it.
- **Sewer Plague**: after each Long Rest you save against it. A failure adds a level of Exhaustion, a success takes one away, and at none it ends. While it holds you, a Long Rest restores no Hit Points.
- **Sight Rot**: magic that ends its blindness, such as Lesser Restoration, ends it.

While a contagion holds your Exhaustion, a Long Rest doesn't lower it.

## The 2014 rules

With **Rules** set to 2014, these use the 2014 versions:

- the conditions: Exhaustion's six levels, Grappled, Incapacitated, Invisible, Stunned and Petrified;
- surprise;
- a Long Rest gives back only half your Hit Dice.

Exhaustion's "Speed halved" halves each of your Speeds.
