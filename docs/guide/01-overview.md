# Welcome to WildPath

WildPath is a Foundry VTT system for the 2024 fifth-edition rules, with some of Baldur's Gate 3's flow. You click an Action, and the system resolves it: it rolls, applies damage and conditions, spends what it costs, and offers reactions.

This guide is in pages:

1. **Playing a turn**: the action bar, targets and areas, reactions, and chat cards.
2. **Your character**: leveling up, Heroic Inspiration, and languages.
3. **Books and homebrew**: content sources, your own books, the builder, and the Rules Inspector.
4. **GM tools**: rests, journeys, chases, Bastions, experience, crafting, traps and contagions.
5. **Exploring and gear**: carrying, coins, light, ammunition, poisons, scrolls and Bastions.
6. **Conditions and rules**: conditions beyond the core ones, contagions, and the 2014 rules.
7. **Settings reference**.

The system's content is the System Reference Document 5.2 (SRD 5.2) by Wizards of the Coast LLC, under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. The books a GM owns can be added to a world for that GM's own table; they never ship with the system.
