# Your character

## Leveling up

The level-up dialog opens when you drop a class, species or background on your sheet, or click **Level up**. It asks for:

- that level's features;
- your choices: skills, training, Weapon Mastery;
- an Ability Score Improvement or a feat;
- a subclass, or a species' size.

Confirm stays disabled until every choice is made, and closing the dialog changes nothing.

**Hit Points.** You get the Hit Die's maximum at level 1. After that, each level gives its average rounded up plus your Fortitude modifier, or a roll if the GM turned on **Roll hit points on level up**.

**Experience.** With experience points, your sheet shows your XP and the XP your next level needs. After a fight, the GM awards the defeated foes' XP, and the chat says when you may level up. With milestones, the GM tells you when.

## Heroic Inspiration

You hold up to 4 Heroic Inspiration points, shown on your sheet and the bar; the GM awards them. When you miss an attack, fail a save or fail a check, you're asked whether to spend a point to reroll it. If the GM allows it, the GM may also spend one of your points to reroll a monster's d20.

## Languages

Wrap words in a language's tag, such as `[elvish]Well met[/elvish]`. This works in chat, journals and speech bubbles, and the editor has a Language menu. In-character chat without a tag is Common.

Who reads it plainly:

- the GM and its author;
- anyone whose creature knows that language;
- anyone under Comprehend Languages, Tongues or Telepathy.

Everyone else sees the same words in a historic script: Elvish in Tifinagh, Dwarvish in runes. A GM can choose fonts instead with **Fonts for languages**.
