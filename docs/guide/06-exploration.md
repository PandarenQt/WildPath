# Exploring and gear

## Carrying

The world's **Carrying** setting decides what a character's load does.

- **Carrying capacity** (the 2024 rule, the default): a character carries up to its Might score × 15 lb, more for a larger creature. If it carries more, its Speed is at most 5 feet.
- **Variant encumbrance**: over 5 × Might the character is Encumbered (−10 feet of Speed). Over 10 × Might it is Heavily Encumbered (−20 feet, and Disadvantage on Might, Agility and Fortitude d20 tests).
- **Off**: weight is only shown.

The sheet's gear list shows what you carry against your capacity, and what the load does to you.

## Coins

Your coins (PP, GP, EP, SP, CP) are with your gear on the sheet. Crafting pays from them and makes change.

## Light

To light a Torch, Candle, Lamp or Lantern, use its **Light** Action. A Torch or Candle lights with a Bonus Action. Your Token then sheds the item's light for its hours: a Torch gives Bright Light 20 feet and Dim Light 20 feet more, and a Bullseye Lantern shines a cone.

## Ammunition

A weapon with the Ammunition property needs ammunition of its kind in your gear: Arrows for a bow, Bolts for a crossbow, Bullets for a sling. Each attack spends one, and with none left the attack is refused. After a fight, the GM may let you recover half of what you spent.

## Poisons

- **Contact, ingested and inhaled poisons**: using one is the Utilize action on a creature within 5 feet, which saves against it.
- **Injury poisons**: using one coats your weapon for 1 minute. Your next weapon hit offers the poison's save to the creature you hit, and that uses up the coat.

## Spell Scrolls

A Spell Scroll in your gear gives you its spell's Action. If you can't cast spells, you can't read it. If the spell's level is above what you can cast, you first make a check with your spellcasting ability, DC 10 + its level, and lose the spell on a failure. Either way, the scroll crumbles once read.

## Unidentified items

Until your GM says you've identified a magic item, you see only what it looks like: its unidentified name and description, also on the Actions it gives you.

## Bastions

From level 5 a character may have a Bastion. Its facilities are listed in the **Bastion** section of your sheet. Before each Bastion turn, issue each special facility its order there; the GM runs the turn.

- **Facilities.** Drop a facility on your sheet. A special one needs its level and a free slot (2 at level 5, 4 at 9, 5 at 13, 6 at 17); the sheet shows how many you use. Your first two basic facilities are free; more cost gold, as does **Enlarge**.
- **Crafting.** Drop an Item on a facility that crafts (a Smithy, a Workshop) to choose what its Craft orders make.
- **Defenders.** The sheet shows your Bastion Defenders and how many your facilities house.
- **The map.** **Bastion map** opens a grid of 5-foot squares, 12 by 12 at level 5, growing to 24 by 24 at 17. Pick a facility, then click or drag over squares to lay it out in any shape, up to its space (Cramped 4 squares, Roomy 16, Vast 36). Right-click clears a square. **Save the map** when it has no problems listed.
